Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 24, 2026

1. Introduction

Retkit ("we", "our", "us") operates an AI-powered assistant platform for hospitality businesses. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you interact with our services, including through Instagram Direct and WhatsApp messaging.

2. Information We Collect

When you message a business using our platform, we collect:

  • Message content you send to the business
  • Your platform user identifier (Instagram or WhatsApp ID)
  • Timestamp of messages

We do not collect your password, payment information, or personal data beyond what you share in conversations.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide AI-assisted responses on behalf of the business
  • To enable human operators to assist you when needed
  • To extract booking or service preferences you share (e.g., name, phone, preferred time)
  • To improve our service quality

4. Data Sharing

We share your conversation data only with the business you are messaging. We do not sell your data to third parties. We use OpenRouter for AI processing — message content is sent to language model providers under their data processing agreements.

5. Data Retention

Conversation data is retained for up to 12 months, after which it is automatically deleted. You may request deletion at any time by contacting us.

6. Data Security

We use encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest. Access to data is restricted to authorized personnel and automated systems.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to your data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Opt out of AI-assisted responses

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries: privacy@retkit.io

9. Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
